# Digital inventory levers (#709)

Evidence for the inventory half of the Power and Area design-change work (#700):
which cell-level reductions in the digital section move the `< 1 uA` idle and
`< 0.05 mm^2` area rows, and by how much.

- [`levers.md`](levers.md) -- the before/after table, generated by
  `python3 sim/tools/inventory_levers.py --write` (`--check` fails when stale).
- [`interface-raw-shift-no-reset.patch`](interface-raw-shift-no-reset.patch) -- the one RTL
  candidate, kept as a patch because it is **not applied** (see below).

## Result

Every priced candidate together takes the projected digital idle from 2.211 uA
to about 2.14 uA and trims the floorplan by about 3 000 um^2. Neither row comes
near `met`; the digital leakage floor is the flop and combinational population
that DR-0017 and DR-0019 already identified. Latch storage is the one lever
with visible reach (about a further 140 nA) and it is not adopted, for the
reasons below.

## Synthesized cross-check of the RTL candidate

The same RTL candidate, run through `design/synth.py`'s own `synthesize()` into
its scratch directory (committed files untouched), before and after applying the patch:

| | instances | area um^2 | `dffq_1` | `dffrnq_1` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| main | 1459 | 57435.84 | 128 | 190 |
| patched | 1395 | 56464.93 | 159 | 158 |

So the synthesized saving of dropping `raw_shift`'s reset and clear is 970.9 um^2
(64 cells), well above the 351 um^2 the inventory prices for the flop swap alone:
the per-bit clear logic disappears with it. `raw_bit_count`'s top bit was already
pruned by synthesis, so the 5-bit counter changes nothing in the netlist.
With the patch applied, `sim/tests/test_interface.py` (including the RTL-versus-model
equivalence run under iverilog), `sim/tests/test_power_rollups.py` and
`npm run check:regmap` all passed.

## Why the RTL candidate is not applied here

Applying it makes `design/trng_top/trng_top.synth.v` stale, and the committed
place-and-route result, its input-hash guard (`npm run check:digital-input`), the
gate-level timing/power records and the floorplan all hang off that exact netlist.
Re-synthesizing would also drag in this host's different `klt` engine metadata.
The change therefore has to ride with a deliberate synthesis plus place-and-route
re-run, which is where #710 (floorplan utilisation) and the power-gating work
already converge. Until then the patch is checked to still apply (`--check`).

## Candidates not attempted, and why

- **Conditioner (41 flops) and health tests (45 flops) width audit.** Counter widths
  are derived from the ratified parameters (`$clog2(C_RCT + 1)`, `$clog2(W + 1)`,
  `$clog2(BLOCK_BITS)`), so each is already the minimum for its range; 41 = 32 CRC
  state + 8 block counter + 1 valid strobe. No reduction without changing DR-0002 or the
  conditioner definition.
- **Interface pointers and counters (candidate 4).** The occupancy counters are
  `TRNG_LEVEL_BITS = 4` wide against a depth-2 need of 2; narrowing is a regmap
  change (`LEVEL_BITS`, DR-0013/DR-0020), so it needs a decision record and is out of scope.
- **FIFO depth and merging the raw FIFO.** Rejected by the issue (DR-0020).
- **Depth-2 read mux structure.** One `mux2_1` per bit is already the smallest 2:1 cell.

## Drift against the issue text

The issue quotes 758.2 uW active at `18d24d1`. At the tree this was built on,
`python3 sim/tools/power_rollup.py` reports a worst active corner of 418.5 uW
(83.7 % of `< 500 uW`) and a worst idle corner of 2.211 uA (221 %), so the
active row reads `met` at that rollup and only the idle row and the area row remain open.
The inventory also lists 8 control flops where the RTL has 9 (`fail_ring`), a
discrepancy outside this issue.
