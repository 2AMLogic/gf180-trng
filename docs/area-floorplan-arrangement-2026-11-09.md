# Floorplan arrangement: stacking the digital block above the analog strip (2026-11-09)

Append-only measurement record for issue #797 (part of #700 via #793). It asks
whether the empty area beside the single-row analog strip in the composed
floorplan can be reclaimed by rearranging the composition, and reports what a
trial stacked arrangement measured. **Outcome: the stack is geometrically
clean but is not landed, because it breaks two downstream gates that cannot
be restored by re-deriving the routing from new origins alone.** No committed
floorplan artefact, generator, spec row or decision record is changed by this
record; the ratified Area row (< 0.05 mm^2) is untouched and its miss stays
disclosed as FAIL.

Area is geometry; no PVT corner applies. The IR-drop figure quoted in section
3 is the existing corner-swept bound from `sim/tools/digital_supply_ir_drop.py`
and keeps its own corners.

## Method

The trial changed only the placement arithmetic in
`layout/floorplan/floorplan.py` (`_row_offsets` and the origins `compose()`
builds from it): the three analog regions keep their left-to-right order as one
strip at `y = 0`; the `digital` region is placed above that strip, one 20 um
isolation channel (DR-0019) above the tallest analog region, with its east edge
at the strip's east edge plus an overhang. The inter-region wiring was
regenerated by `layout/floorplan/interregion.py` from the new origins; nothing
was hand-edited. The trial was then discarded (the tree is unchanged).

Tool builds. The committed `layout/floorplan/reports/` and
`trng_floorplan.gds` are normative under `klayout-tools==0.6.0` with
`klayout==0.30.10` (DR-0026 as amended), so the floorplan flow was run from a
throwaway virtual environment at that pin, not the host `klt` (a dev build,
`0.9.2.dev385`, which does not reproduce the committed stream: it measures a
932.3 um wide bbox against the committed 931.145 um and reports composed DRC
violations on the unmodified tree). `signoff/check.py` was run under
`klayout-tools==0.8.0`, the grader pin in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. <!-- klt-pin: grader --> No host
tool was installed or changed.

## 3. Why it is not landed: two downstream gates break

Both were run on the 6 um trial tree after regenerating the floorplan
artefacts (`--write`) and re-pinning `signoff/` (`check.py --write`, klt
0.8.0), and compared against an unmodified export of `HEAD`.

1. **`python3 sim/tools/digital_supply_ir_drop.py --check` (part of
   `check:spec`) fails.** Committed tree: active bound 23.180 mV, idle bound
   0.515 mV, both below the 33.000 mV materiality yardstick -- OK. Trial tree:
   `active: cross-corner bound 69.001 mV reaches the 33.000 mV materiality
   yardstick`, and `characterization-digital-supply-ir-drop.md` goes stale.
   What changed on the path (derived from `interregion.digital_pdn_strap_bands`
   and the placement origins, not from a re-run of the IR model): the
   `vddd`/`vss` docking path (#224) is a single 0.30 um Metal3 riser with one
   Via3 at each end, from the trunk under the strip up to the lowest Metal5 PDN
   strap. That strap sits at local y = 97.4-101.9 um (`vddd`) and 52.6-57.1 um
   (`vss`), so the riser is already long in the committed row (about 110 um and
   67 um). The stack lifts the digital block by 38.25 um, which lengthens both
   risers by that amount (about 149 um and 105 um); the `vss` trunk is not
   longer (430.2 um against 438.8 um committed). This record did not isolate
   which term drives the 23.2 -> 69.0 mV change, so the riser length is a
   candidate cause, not an established one.
   A wide, multi-cut riser on a different path is the likely remedy, which is a
   redesign of the PDN tie rather than a re-derivation from origins. Relaxing
   the yardstick is a spec question and is not done here.
2. **The tie also moves into the entropy-source channel.** The trial's risers
   land at x = 99.865 (`vss`) and 100.865 (`vddd`) um: inside the
   `ring1 | ring2` isolation channel (x = 80.91-100.91), the `vddd` riser at
   `ring2`'s guard edge. The committed row routes them only in the
   `combiner_sampler | digital` channel. DRC and LVS do not see this, but it
   puts digital supply current beside the entropy rings that
   `layout/floorplan/README.md` exists to isolate from it, so it needs an
   isolation argument, not just a clean DRC.
3. **`python3 -m unittest` under `npm run test:layout` fails** on
   `test_interregion.TrunkLanes.test_clk_and_rst_n_never_run_west_of_the_combiner_sampler_region`
   (`net clk runs west of the combiner_sampler region`, 88.115 against a
   203.32 floor). It encodes the row ordering; updating it is part of any real
   re-arrangement, but it is a guard on the interregion routing, not an
   incidental assertion.

`check:signoff` (klt 0.8.0), `check:layout`, `check:floorplan`,
`check:floorplan-netlist`, `check:artifact-manifests` and
`check:record-checksums` passed on the trial tree after regeneration.
`check:signoff` needs the re-pin because the signoff inventory hashes
`trng_floorplan.gds`; the verdict of record did not change (18/22 items met).

## 4. Consumers identified as stale if an arrangement did land

- `layout/pex/` and the `sim/tb/*-extracted-fullchip` testbenches that
  reference `trng_floorplan` (`ro-array-core-power`, `ro-array-core-startup`,
  `ro-array-core-pvt-q`, `sampler-core-idle-leakage`, with the records dated
  2026-09-12 and 2026-10-30) use extracted full-chip inputs built from the
  composed stream; any accepted re-arrangement makes them stale. They are not
  re-simulated here (simulation is opt-in).
- `layout/floorplan/README.md`, `signoff/inventories.py` (it pins the stream's
  hash), `layout/floorplan/reports/*.json` and `signoff/block-manifest.json`
  change with the stream.

## 5. What a landable arrangement needs

Tracked in #803 (not this record): redesign the `digital` PDN tie in
`layout/floorplan/interregion.py` for a stacked placement so that (a) the
`vddd`/`vss` risers are wide and multi-cut enough to keep the digital supply
bound under the 33 mV yardstick, (b) they run in a channel that is argued
against the isolation rationale rather than the `ring1 | ring2` channel, and
(c) the east-edge riser columns of `combiner_sampler` and the digital
top-pin wrap are separated by construction rather than by an overhang constant.
The trial patch is small (about 30 lines in `floorplan.py`: a
`_stack_origins()` helper, an east-overhang constant, and `compose()` using
2-D origins) and the placement half is reproducible from the description in
the Method section.

```sh
  "klayout-tools==0.6.0" "klayout==0.30.10"
PATH=$PWD/.venv-klt/bin:$PATH python3 layout/floorplan/floorplan.py
PATH=$PWD/.venv-klt/bin:$PATH klt economy layout/floorplan/trng_floorplan.gds
```

The first two commands reproduce the committed-row column (PASS, artefacts
match); the stacked column requires the placement change described above.
